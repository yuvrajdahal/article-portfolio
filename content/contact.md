I read everything that arrives. If you're hiring, have a system that needs building, or want a second pair of eyes on an architecture, send a note — a couple of sentences about the problem is plenty to start.

**Useful things to include:** what you're building, where it runs today, and what's currently in the way. If there's a repository or a spec, link it.

I usually reply within a day.
